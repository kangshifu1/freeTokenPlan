# OpenRouter

[中文](./README.md) | [English](./README.en.md)

- Website: [OpenRouter Quickstart](https://openrouter.ai/docs/quickstart)
- Registration: email or social login, then create an API key
- Free tier: many `:free` models and no credit card required for a free account; the current official limits page lists `20 requests/min`, `50 requests/day` for accounts with less than `$10` purchased credits, and `1000 requests/day` after purchasing at least `$10` credits
- Routing: use a specific `:free` model, or use `openrouter/free` to let OpenRouter choose from currently available free models
- Recommended for: model sampling, OpenAI-compatible routing, quick testing
